ABOUT SOLUTUS

MOUND, MINNESOTA · ESTABLISHED 2020

A small business.
A personal commitment.

Solutus LLC is a one-person company based in the western suburbs of Minneapolis / St. Paul, with access to a network of professionals available when a project calls for additional resources.

Solutus specializes in mobile and web applications for small and medium-sized businesses, helping improve automation and access to information. We also build static and dynamic websites for personal and professional use.

You work directly with the owner from the first conversation through the build and launch. That means personal attention, clear communication, and guidance tailored to your goals, budget, and comfort with technology. Decisions about how your site or app will be hosted, released, and maintained are part of the conversation from the start.

Cory Peterson, Owner

From writing BASIC on a Commodore 64 to exploring new hardware and software, AI and beyond, technology has always been a passion. Experience in service, sales, B2B brand building, and management brings a practical understanding of the challenges businesses face.
